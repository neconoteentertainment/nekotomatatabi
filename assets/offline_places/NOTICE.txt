Each gzip cell is UTF-8 JSON rows:
[OSM type/id, name, latitude, longitude, category index].
Category names, cell file names/counts and SHA-256 hashes are in manifest.json.
Rows are deduplicated by name and coordinates rounded to 5 decimals.
Ways/relations use bounding-box centers (not entrance or boundary geometry).
Names prefer name:ja, then name, then official_name. Objects without names
are omitted. The selection follows the app's existing Overpass tag filters:
tourism attraction/museum/gallery/zoo/aquarium/viewpoint/hotel/guest_house/theme_park;
amenity place_of_worship/arts_centre; historic; leisure park/garden;
castle_type; shop department_store/mall. Matching follows the existing regex.
Data is not exhaustive and may include closed, incomplete or inaccurate places.
